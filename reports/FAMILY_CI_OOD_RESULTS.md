# Family-Level / CI Analysis: OOD Results

This note summarizes the confidence-interval extension for `domain_ood` and `structural_ood` generated by `scripts/family_ci_analysis.py`.

## 1. Domain OOD Confidence Intervals

`domain_ood.csv` does not expose a family identifier, so these intervals use row bootstrap rather than family bootstrap.

- DeBERTa-v3: F1 `0.9834`, 95% CI `[0.9777, 0.9890]`
- DistilBERT: F1 `0.9055`, 95% CI `[0.8908, 0.9185]`
- TF-IDF + LR: F1 `0.9508`, 95% CI `[0.9412, 0.9607]`
- Rule-based: F1 `0.1326`, 95% CI `[0.1047, 0.1592]`

The domain-OOD ranking is therefore statistically clear at the benchmark level: DeBERTa-v3 remains strongest, DistilBERT is materially weaker, and the rule-based floor is very poor.

## 2. Structural OOD Confidence Intervals

`structural_ood.csv` supports a family-based view using `label:original_seed_idx` as the unit. The primary structural metric is benign FPR.

Row-level benign FPR with family-bootstrap 95% CI:

- DeBERTa-v3: FPR `0.8248`, 95% CI `[0.7889, 0.8544]`
- DistilBERT: FPR `0.7317`, 95% CI `[0.7065, 0.7558]`
- TF-IDF + LR: FPR `0.7918`, 95% CI `[0.7523, 0.8304]`
- Rule-based: FPR `0.3223`, 95% CI `[0.2799, 0.3677]`

This confirms that structural benign false positives are not a small-sample artifact.

## 3. Family-Level Sensitivity On Structural OOD

Collapsing structural rows to the family level changes the measured benign FPR:

- DeBERTa-v3: row FPR `0.8248` -> family FPR `0.8498`
- DistilBERT: row FPR `0.7317` -> family FPR `0.8378`
- TF-IDF + LR: row FPR `0.7918` -> family FPR `0.8258`
- Rule-based: row FPR `0.3223` -> family FPR `0.2312`

The most important effect is on DistilBERT: structural over-defense looks even worse when collapsed to the family level.

## Reviewer-Facing Lesson

These intervals strengthen two paper claims:

1. The domain-OOD performance ranking is stable enough to report without hedging away the differences.
2. The structural-OOD benign FPR problem is large and statistically stable, including under family-level sensitivity.
