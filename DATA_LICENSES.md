# Data sources and licenses

PIDS-Bench is assembled from several public datasets plus material we constructed
ourselves. This file records, for every value in the `source` column, its upstream
dataset and governing license, so the provenance of the benchmark is fully
traceable.

The code in this repository is released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). The
**data** is not MIT; it inherits the terms of its upstream sources, summarized
below. Because two sources are non-commercial (Alpaca and qualifire, both CC BY-NC
4.0), the benchmark as a whole should be treated as **research / non-commercial use
only**.

## Source naming

Two `source` values use a label that differs from their upstream dataset. The
mapping is fixed and is given here so provenance is unambiguous:

- `sharegpt` denotes prompter messages from **OpenAssistant/oasst1**.
- `stackexchange` denotes instructions from **databricks/databricks-dolly-15k**.

These labels are stable across the v3 release; the source-by-source table below
lists the upstream dataset and license for each.

## How restricted text is handled

Most sources are redistributed in full. Two cases are handled differently:

1. **LMSYS-Chat-1M rows are not redistributed.** The LMSYS license does not permit
   third-party redistribution of the conversation text. The verbatim LMSYS rows in
   `hard_benign_test.csv` and `structural_ood.csv` therefore have their `text`
   column removed from the public files. They can be reconstructed locally with
   `rebuild_restricted.py` by anyone who has accepted the LMSYS license; the
   reconstruction is verified row-by-row against `restricted_rebuild_manifest.csv`,
   which stores only SHA-256 fingerprints (no text).

2. **Alpaca- and OASST-seeded training rows are paraphrases, not source text.** The
   `alpaca_benign` and `sharegpt` rows in `train/val/test` were rewritten by
   gpt-4o-mini (one to three paraphrase rounds; see the `paraphrase_round` and
   `generator_model` columns). The text in the released files is model-generated
   derivative text, not the original Alpaca or OASST text. It is retained in the
   release, attributed to its seed source, and governed by the more restrictive of
   the seed license and the generating model's terms.
